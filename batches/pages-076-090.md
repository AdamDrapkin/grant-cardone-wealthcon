# Who invests in PE?

**Typical Investors** *(green header box)*

PE fund managers (General Partners or GPs) typically raise capital from institutional investors (Limited Partners of LPs):

- Pension funds (retirement savings for employees, ex. CALPERS)
- Insurance companies (premiums from policyholders)
- Endowments (universities, hospitals, non-profits)
- Sovereign wealth funds (government owned funds that invest national reserves)
- Family Offices
- Fund of Funds
- UHNW individuals

**Barriers to Entry** *(red header box)*

- Access to managers
- Once you have access, getting priority and your requested allocation
- Minimum investments are often very high: $5-25MM+
- Requires significant deal flow and pipeline
- Cost of due diligence and ongoing management
- J-Curve and Illiquidity Premium enable higher returns but require a longer time horizon
