# 10X WealthCon (10X Wealth Conference) — Research Brief

Research conducted via public web search, September 2026, for the WealthCon notebook/voice-memo digitization project. Every claim below is followed by its source. Where something could not be verified through public sources, that is stated explicitly rather than inferred or guessed.

**Important caveat on sourcing:** Nearly all detailed information about this specific event (speaker descriptions, quotes, "success" framing) traces back to press releases self-issued by Grant Cardone's organization via PR Newswire/PRWeb and syndicated verbatim by aggregator sites (Yahoo Finance, KXAN "press releases" section, ITNewsOnline, etc.). I was unable to locate independent journalism (a news outlet's own reporting, with a byline and independent verification) or third-party attendee reviews specifically about this event. This is flagged inline wherever it applies.

---

## Exact dates

The inaugural 10X Wealth Conference ("WealthCon") was held **November 17–18, 2024**.

- [10X Wealth Conference, Hosted by Grant Cardone, Announces Impressive and Unparalleled Speaker Line Up](https://www.prweb.com/releases/10x-wealth-conference-hosted-by-grant-cardone-announces-impressive-and-unparalleled-speaker-line-up-302296720.html) (PRWeb press release, syndicated) — states the conference date as November 17–18.
- Corroborated by a mirrored copy of the same release on [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/10x-wealth-conference-hosted-grant-170000807.html).
- A Grant Cardone post on X dated around November 12, 2024 references an imminent "10X Wealth Conference" and ties a promotional ticket giveaway to "Nov 15" pricing — consistent with a mid/late-November date: [Grant Cardone on X](https://x.com/GrantCardone/status/1856160289688834277).

I did not find an independent (non-Cardone-affiliated) news source that separately confirms the exact dates; the date comes from the organizer's own press materials, corroborated across multiple syndication points and the organizer's own social media, which increases confidence even though the underlying source is self-published.

## City and venue

Held at the **JW Marriott Miami Turnberry Resort & Spa**, in **Aventura, Florida** (part of the greater Miami metro area — press materials interchangeably describe the location as "Miami" and "Aventura").

- [10X Wealth Conference, Hosted by Grant Cardone, Announces Impressive and Unparalleled Speaker Line Up](https://www.prweb.com/releases/10x-wealth-conference-hosted-by-grant-cardone-announces-impressive-and-unparalleled-speaker-line-up-302296720.html) — names the JW Marriott Turnberry venue.
- [1st Annual 10X Wealth Conference Hosted by Grant Cardone: A Transformational Gathering for Financial Growth and Innovation](https://www.prweb.com/releases/1st-annual-10x-wealth-conference-hosted-by-grant-cardone-a-transformational-gathering-for-financial-growth-and-innovation-302311739.html) (post-event recap release) — same venue/location.
- The venue's real-world identity (JW Marriott Miami Turnberry Resort & Spa, located in Aventura, FL) is independently confirmed by [Wikipedia: JW Marriott Miami Turnberry Resort & Spa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/JW_Marriott_Miami_Turnberry_Resort_%26_Spa), which is not Cardone-affiliated and simply verifies the hotel exists at that location — it does not independently confirm Cardone's event was held there.
- Grant Cardone himself posted from the venue: "At 10X Wealth Conference in Miami – come by" ([X post](https://x.com/GrantCardone/status/1999499679860392290) — note: this specific status ID resolves oddly/may be a later or misdated post; treat as supporting but not conclusive).

## Format

Described in organizer materials as a **two-day event** (November 17–18) structured around keynote-style speaker sessions and **moderated "fireside chats."**

- [10X Wealth Conference, Hosted by Grant Cardone, Announces Impressive and Unparalleled Speaker Line Up](https://www.prweb.com/releases/10x-wealth-conference-hosted-by-grant-cardone-announces-impressive-and-unparalleled-speaker-line-up-302296720.html) — describes "two days of actionable insights, networking, and strategies" and states Fox Business's Charlie Gasparino "moderated the event's fireside chats."
- [1st Annual 10X Wealth Conference recap release](https://www.prweb.com/releases/1st-annual-10x-wealth-conference-hosted-by-grant-cardone-a-transformational-gathering-for-financial-growth-and-innovation-302311739.html) — describes it as "two days of dynamic networking, insightful discussions, and actionable strategies."
- Topic breadth per the same release: real estate, private equity, stock markets, and tax strategy sessions were all represented, aimed at "everyday investors."

No independently reported minute-by-minute agenda, session count, or room/track structure was found. I could not verify a detailed session schedule beyond "two days, speaker talks plus fireside-chat-style moderated conversations" — **could not verify further structural detail (e.g., number of concurrent tracks, exact session times) via public sources as of this research.**

## Grant Cardone's role

Cardone is described consistently across all sources as the **host and organizer** of the event — it is his company's branded conference (part of the "10X Events" portfolio under Cardone Training Technologies, Inc. / Cardone Enterprises), and he is also presented as a headline speaker himself.

- [10X Wealth Conference, Hosted by Grant Cardone...](https://www.prweb.com/releases/10x-wealth-conference-hosted-by-grant-cardone-announces-impressive-and-unparalleled-speaker-line-up-302296720.html) — Cardone described in the speaker lineup as a "real estate fund manager with $4.8 billion AUM" and the event's host.
- [1st Annual 10X Wealth Conference recap](https://www.prweb.com/releases/1st-annual-10x-wealth-conference-hosted-by-grant-cardone-a-transformational-gathering-for-financial-growth-and-innovation-302311739.html) — quotes Cardone: "We are thrilled with the success of the first annual 10X Wealth Conference," and states plans for a second annual event were already underway.
- Cardone's broader corporate umbrella (Cardone Enterprises / CTTI, which includes Cardone University, Cardone Capital, Cardone Ventures, 10X Events, and the Grant Cardone Foundation) is described on [Cardone Ventures' Team page](https://cardoneventures.com/team/) and referenced in coverage of a related Cardone conference in [Aventura Community News](https://communitynewspapers.com/aventura-news/cardone-enterprisestaking-business-and-philanthropy-worldwide/) (a local community newspaper — one of the few pieces of coverage in this research that is not a Cardone-issued press release, though it covers the broader Cardone Enterprises organization rather than this specific event).

Note: the $4.8 billion AUM figure and similar Cardone-supplied statistics about his own funds are self-reported in his own promotional material; they are not independently verified here, and given Cardone's public legal history around investment-return claims (see below), such figures should be treated as claims, not verified fact.

## Other confirmed speakers (identification of garbled names)

### Karlton Dennis / "Tax Alchemy" — CONFIRMED, spelling corrected
The correct spelling is **Karlton Dennis** (not "Carlton"), founder/CEO of **Tax Alchemy**, a tax strategy firm. He is described in event coverage as a tax strategist speaking on tax legislation and tax efficiency.
- [Tax Alchemy | Licensed Tax Strategists](https://taxalchemy.com/) and [Karlton Dennis bio, Tax Alchemy](https://taxalchemy.com/karlton-dennis/) — confirms name spelling and role.
- Search results summarizing the WealthCon speaker-lineup press release describe "Karlton Dennis... recognized as the #1 Tax Strategist in the U.S., having saved clients over $100 million," with his WealthCon session focused on "navigating tax legislation and maximizing tax efficiency" — drawn from [10X Wealth Conference speaker lineup release](https://www.prweb.com/releases/10x-wealth-conference-hosted-by-grant-cardone-announces-impressive-and-unparalleled-speaker-line-up-302296720.html).
- Also listed as a speaker at other finance events, e.g. [Alternative Asset Investor Summit](https://altassetsummit.com/speaker/karlton-dennis/), which independently confirms he is an actively booked conference speaker in this space (not proof of the WealthCon booking specifically, but corroborates plausibility).

**Confidence: high** that "Carlton Dennis / Tax Alchemy" in the notes = Karlton Dennis of Tax Alchemy; this is very likely just a spelling slip in transcription, not a different person.

### "Gary Cohn" — CONFIRMED
**Gary Cohn** did speak at WealthCon 2024, per the organizer's speaker-lineup press release, and is independently a real, well-known public figure matching the "former Trump economic advisor" description in the notes.
- [10X Wealth Conference speaker lineup release](https://www.prweb.com/releases/10x-wealth-conference-hosted-by-grant-cardone-announces-impressive-and-unparalleled-speaker-line-up-302296720.html) — lists Gary Cohn, described there as (at the time) Vice Chairman of IBM, discussing economic policy and market dynamics.
- Independent biographical confirmation that Gary Cohn served as **Director of the National Economic Council / chief economic advisor to President Trump, 2017–2018**, after a long career as President of Goldman Sachs: [Wikipedia: Gary Cohn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gary_Cohn); [Forbes: Gary Cohn Is Leaving Goldman Sachs To Chair Trump's National Economic Council](https://www.forbes.com/sites/antoinegara/2016/12/12/gary-cohn-is-leaving-goldman-sachs-to-chair-donald-trumps-national-economic-council/); [Washington Post coverage of his NEC appointment](https://www.washingtonpost.com/news/wonk/wp/2016/12/09/trump-to-name-goldman-sachs-veteran-gary-cohn-to-head-national-economic-council/).
- Grant Cardone himself posted about appearing with Cohn: "Gary Cohn and I discuss WEALTH" ([X post](https://twitter.com/GrantCardone/status/1858261865627091197), dated mid-November 2024, consistent with the event window).

**Confidence: high** — this is the real Gary Cohn, correctly identified.

## Grant Cardone's broader legal/regulatory record (context, not event-specific)

Per the task's instructions, noting this factually and neutrally: Grant Cardone and Cardone Capital have an ongoing public legal history concerning investor-return claims. I found no indication this WealthCon event itself is named in any lawsuit or SEC action — the following is general background context, not specific to the November 2024 WealthCon:

- A federal appeals court (Ninth Circuit) revived a class-action lawsuit against Cardone and Cardone Capital on **June 10, 2025**, finding investors may have been misled by promises of a 15% return; the ruling cites an SEC letter warning Cardone against including unsupported projected returns in offering materials. [Court revives lawsuit over 15% fund return promise — InvestmentNews](https://www.investmentnews.com/regulation-legal-compliance/court-revives-lawsuit-over-15-fund-return-promise/260910); [Grant Cardone's Previously Dismissed Lawsuit Is Back — Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/grant-cardones-previously-dismissed-lawsuit-183034328.html); [Cardone Equity Fund Class Action Lawsuit summary — Migliaccio & Rathod / classlawgroup.com](https://www.classlawgroup.com/cardone-equity-fund-class-action-lawsuit).
- Reporting on the case states actual investor distributions were reportedly far below the amounts projected, and that audited filings show net losses in at least one Cardone REIT vehicle (cited losses of $9.75M in FY2024 and $5.26M in FY2025 for "Cardone REIT I"). [USA Herald: Grant Cardone's 15% Investment Hype Faces Major Legal Reversal](https://usaherald.com/grant-cardones-15-investment-hype-faces-major-legal-reversal/); [Investor Claims: Grant Cardone Lawsuit Revived After 15% Returns Promised](https://investorclaims.com/blog/grant-cardone-lawsuit-revived/).
- A separate, later development: a former Cardone Capital Chief Marketing Officer reportedly filed a $1 billion defamation suit against Cardone in connection with testimony given in a related investor case (Pino v. Cardone) — this is unrelated to WealthCon itself but part of the same general legal-controversy landscape. [USA Herald: Cardone Brothers Face Legal Storm](https://usaherald.com/cardone-brothers-face-legal-storm-appeals-court-revives-major-class-action-against-grant-while-gary-battles-separate-investment-allegations/).

None of these sources tie the litigation to any specific statement made at the November 2024 WealthCon event; this section is background context per the task instructions, not a claim about anything said at WealthCon itself.
